Store Management

Add equipment here and publish it directly to the customer Store. Products marked Published appear on product.html.

Media Upload

Upload product images and promotional videos directly from the admin dashboard. Files are stored in Supabase Storage bucket tdm-media.

If upload says the bucket does not exist, create a public Supabase Storage bucket named tdm-media and allow authenticated admins to upload.

Front Board

In Automatic mode, the newest published product replaces the previous front-board product automatically. Switch to Custom Advertisement when you want to advertise something else.

Shipping & Checkout Fees

These rates control the checkout page. Transportation = base fee + distance × per-km fee. Packaging and clearance are added separately.

Address / Zone Overrides

Example: match text Lagos or Port Harcourt. If the customer's address contains the match text, that zone's transportation and clearance rates are used.
