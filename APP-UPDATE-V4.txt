TDM Manufacturing App V4

Changes:
- Added visible TDM logo/brand header to customer pages.
- Store product cards now show only Contact Us and Add to Cart.
- Removed the Home-page Request a Quote button.
- Added in-app affiliate center under Account. Customers can join, receive an app-generated referral code, copy/share a referral link, and referral codes are captured during signup.
- Affiliate participation no longer requires WhatsApp.
- Updated service worker cache version to force the new HTML/header/navigation to refresh.

Note: referral attribution is carried into Supabase Auth user metadata. A full commission/earnings ledger can be added later with a dedicated affiliate database table and admin controls.
